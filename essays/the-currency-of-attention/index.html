The currency of attention

The opening here, 1 minThe whole essay is in the book

The grass is greener where you choose to water it. This is an analogy for where you place your attention, your awareness and your consciously deliberate thinking: on the parts of life that matter to you. A professional diver is more rehearsed in their sport than the average person simply because of this concept. Even on their off days, because they love it, they cannot help but rehearse it in their mind, studying the motions and the fluidity of their entry into the water, thinking about it frequently.

It is important to see just how far regular attention towards a particular way of life, activity or concept can take a person. We are all born with the finite asset of time, and with it we allocate our currency of attention to the key aspects of life. To choose deliberately where to allocate that time is the game of life itself. The why is the necessary precursor, because it justifies investing your most important currencies, your time and attention, in those activities.

This is why every single day could be viewed as a real blessing, but only if you deliberately choose to live your days with intention and do not drift through the hours without regard for how you are spending your god-given currency.
