The step aside

The opening here, 1 minThe whole essay is in the book

Nobody teaches you to step aside. Not your parents, not your school, not the system that shaped them both. You are taught to push, to chase, to accumulate. And so you do, until one day you are sitting on the floor next to your bed with your eyes closed, and the silence tells you something the world never did: you do not have to want so many things.

What brought me to that floor was the realisation that I had been buying into the experience of life rather than creating it, letting life happen to me rather than for me. As a result, I could not see the idiosyncrasies available to me at every turn, the small adjustments and shifts in perspective that were mine to make if only I would stop and look. When you sit in that silence, you let the imagination wash over you. You channel your creative vision and watch what it is you truly intend to do with your time in this world.

And in doing so, you begin to relinquish your forms of suffering, because you realise you are the thinker of the thoughts, not the thoughts themselves. You can choose.
