Privacy

Nothing about you is kept that doesn't need to be

No cookies, no tracking, no selling. What little is collected, and why, in plain words.

Last updated 7 November 2026

This website

soulsensis.com is a plain website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Nothing on it asks for your email to read anything. Its fonts are served from soulsensis.com itself, so no font company sees your visit. An essay with a video shows the video's picture, which your browser fetches from YouTube; the video itself loads from YouTube's no-cookie player only when you press play.

The seven statements page (soulsensis.com/seven) runs entirely in your browser. What you type is matched against a list of themes on your own device; it is never sent to this site or to anyone else, never stored, and it is gone when you close the page.

Leaving a question at soulsensis.com/question opens your own email app with the question in it; nothing is sent until you send it from there. It reaches hello@soulsensis.com and is kept only there. A question may be answered in public, as an essay or a video, with anything that could identify you taken out. Your email address is used only to reply.

On the book's page you can ask to be told when the book is out. You do that by sending an email to hello@soulsensis.com. The author keeps your address for that one email and nothing else, it goes to no mailing service, and you can ask for it to be removed at any time.

A conversation

A conversation at soulsensis.com/conversation is booked on Cal.com and paid on Stripe. Cal.com asks for a name, an email address and a time; any name you are comfortable with is fine. Stripe takes the card payment, and soulsensis never sees your card details. Cal.com's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy cover what they keep.

soulsensis keeps the booking (the name you gave, your email, the time and the payment record) for as long as Australian tax law needs, which is five years, and uses your email only for that booking. The conversation is by voice, with no camera. It is not recorded, and nothing you say is written down with your name or email beside it.

Ask soulsensis

Ask soulsensis, the A$5 a month chat, is not open yet. Before it opens, this page will say exactly what it keeps and how to delete it.

soulsensis studio

soulsensis studio is a private desktop tool used by the soulsensis writer, one person, to publish their own videos and Shorts, after approving each one, to their own accounts: the soulsensis YouTube channel, the soulsensis TikTok account, and the soulsensis Instagram and Facebook accounts. It has no other users, no public sign-up and no advertising. This page covers the tool and every account it connects to.

YouTube API Services

soulsensis studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, its user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

YouTube. With the account owner's permission (Google sign-in) the tool uploads the owner's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, schedules when they go public, and posts the first comment on them. It reads the channel's own playlists and the channel's own view and retention figures for the owner's videos. Scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and youtube.force-ssl.

TikTok. With the account owner's permission (TikTok sign-in) the tool sends the owner's own Short to the owner's own TikTok account, as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post the owner has just approved. It reads the account's display name and the posting options TikTok offers it. It does not read the owner's feed, messages, followers or any other person's data.

Instagram and Facebook. With the account owner's permission the tool publishes the owner's own Reels and Shorts to the owner's own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page, and nothing else.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private settings file. The tool keeps the ids and links of the videos and posts it created on the owner's own accounts (so later videos join the same playlists and each Short can link to its full essay), and a log of what was sent and when. Nothing is sent to any server run by soulsensis. No data about viewers or any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared. The tool shows no advertising and builds no profile of anyone.

Google user data

soulsensis studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to publish the owner's own content, never for advertising, never to train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and is never transferred to anyone else.

TikTok and Meta data

Information received from TikTok, Instagram and Facebook is used only to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts and to show whether a post went through. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Use of those services is subject to their own terms and policies: the TikTok Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the Instagram and Facebook privacy policies.

Cookies

soulsensis studio is a desktop tool; it places no cookies and uses no tracking of any kind. This website sets none either.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and Instagram and Facebook under Settings, Apps and websites. Once access is removed the stored token can no longer be used, and the tool deletes data it received from that service within seven days. The token can also be deleted at any time by removing it from the tool's settings file on the owner's computer, and deleting the tool's data folder deletes everything else it keeps. To ask for deletion, write to the address below; it is done within seven days. Deleting the tool's data does not delete the videos already published on the platforms themselves.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to have a booking or a question deleted: hello@soulsensis.com. It is done within seven days, apart from what tax law says must be kept.
