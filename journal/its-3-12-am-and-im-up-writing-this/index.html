It's 3:12 am and I'm up writing this

18 January 2020

I really should fix my sleeping schedule but it's already fucked so can't hurt to fuck it up even more right? Nah, seriously though I just love the night so much, is there really anything wrong with that?

I'm well read with the health benefits of waking up early, starting the day early and hitting the gym, etc… but god damn i, I just love being nocturnal. The night is SO calm, so damn quiet and I love just sitting here on the kitchen table writing whatever the fuck comes to my head for whoever the fuck wants to read it AHAHHAHAA. Nah seriously though - there's something beautiful about the night. Is it the calmness, the silence, the dazed feeling I get from sleep deprivation? Or is it the mixture of all that?

It's definitely the silence. I mean, you can find plenty of places during the day that are silent too because you know that it's not TRULY silent because people are hustling, working, networking, getting shit done… and here I am at 3:15AM writing this and not being sure who's gonna be even bothered reading it.

Actually, can you please leave a comment below if you read my posts on the regular? If you irregularly read them don't bother cus you're not special to me lol. Kidding, let me know anyway haha I honestly don't care. I'm doing this more for myself at this point, I just hope it adds value to people in one way or another because it certainly does for me… uwu.

So.. Where was I? Oh yeah the night - I love it. I love gaming at night as well. I love journaling, well, this post is kinda like a journal too so I guess I got my dose of journaling out of the way for today. I love just being by myself as I realise that I'm embracing the nerdy introvert that I am. Around people I'm extroverted as fuck, and I actually love it, but it drains me man. Being alone is good. It's so good. I use to hate the fact that I was introverted, you see all the alpha male chads out there doing their thing and you're just here being shy and don't know what to say (does anyone get my reference of "chad" ;D). But yeah, I'm glad I embrace the fact I'm introverted but play it off better. When I'm shy… I tell people I'm shy. When I don't know what to say… I tell people I don't know what to say. The way I got out of hating my introvertedness was just letting people know that I'm actually a bit goofy and weird, nothing wrong with that? Like, honesty is the best policy. Just don't fk around and be cool with who you are, unless you're actually retarded - in that case seek help (like myself, wait what? Self roast?)

Goodnight guys I need sleep now xx
