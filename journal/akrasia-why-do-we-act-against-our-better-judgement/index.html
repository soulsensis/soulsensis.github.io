Akrasia: why do we act against our better judgement?

26 April 2020

Akrasia: "the state of mind in which someone acts against their better judgment through weakness of will"

Whilst this phenomenon is perhaps the reason why the world isn't as good as it should be, it's reassuring to know that it is a real thing, a tangible reality that stems from its roots in psychology. It's liberating in a sense to be reminded that you're human, you're fallible and vulnerable to such weaknesses. Further, to improve from your current state of existence, you need to be able to identify what it is that you are exactly suffering from such that you're self-aware enough to improve.

A key theme of self-improvement is to actually face reality and to acknowledge what it is that's wrong with you. Before you're able to get your shit together, you need to face the hard facts that you don't have your shit together. Either your room's a fucking mess, you have no tangible goals, your relationship with your parents is non-existent, you're in a toxic relationship where you're just in it for the sex, you're constantly seeking approval from friends to fuel your narcissism, or perhaps you just know something is wrong with you but you're constantly choosing to turn a blind eye to it. Sound personal? You bet, although perhaps I'm just projecting here :(

Now enters akrasia. In my pursuit of self-improvement, I have noticed two key recurring themes that correlate to its success which are embedded into akrasia's very definition: 1. gut intuition (for better judgement) and 2. discipline (to eliminate weakness of will).

Gut intuition:

As someone relatively neurotic this is fucking hard to hone down, man. I'm emotionally volatile, quite spontaneous, and prone to mood swings which makes it damn hard to have a solid and reliable gut intuition on a lot of things. Further, going through anxiety/depression doesn't help too as I'd often overthink which often clouds my better judgement on so many things. Anyways, how is this relevant to you? So, and please, if anyone can give me further research on this I'd be elated, the gut intuition or instinct is perhaps one of the most important life skills we're all, either consciously or unconsciously, working on.

A 'gut' feeling comes from a balance of logic and emotional understanding, perhaps correlating to a combination of IQ and EQ. I think, more often than not, it is reliable to go with your gut instinct over a decision that is purely based on logic or emotion. The outcome of going with your gut, if you train it right, is that your decisions are often more nuanced and well-balanced as a result of accommodating for a holistic set of variables. These variables may include your personality, your values, logic, and emotional factors. What often clouds me from going with my gut feeling is overthinking as it leads me to do things based on too much logic as opposed to what I intrinsically feel to be right. As such it may end up in regret.

I'm sorry I don't have any research backing on this and it feels like my explanation of our gut intuition has been pulled completely out of my arse, but honestly though, I really believe that this is a skill that we're all working on and should try to improve as we age. Being able to make better decisions is at the end of the day quite subjective because what is 'better' to you is completely different from someone else. Sure, there are objective standards that we are all obligated socially to adhere to but at the end of the day whether you're happy or not with your decision will be based on having a strong ability to 'go with your gut', so to speak.

Linking this to the Ancient Greek aphorism 'know thyself' puts the responsibility on us to know and stay true to our values, our personalities, our use of IQ and EQ to synthesise decisions that are in alignment to our gut intuition. Gah, I feel like what I've just written is all bullshit but please tell me this makes sense LOL.

Discipline:

Oh man, discipline – I could always do with more of this, haha. I don't think I need to explain so much what this is but I'll write a few my thoughts on it. Discipline is the lifeblood of progress and improvement. No matter how good your gut intuition/judgement is, without discipline and the will to execute... you're basically fucked LOL. If you're a thinker but don't execute, I'd argue that perhaps there's nothing more tragic than seeing talent go to waste. It truly is a sad sight to see, like seriously – imagine all that potential just budding inside your brain but you can't get off the couch to fucking do anything about it - argh, this hits a bit too close to home.

Yeah, discipline guys, get on it – it's the conquest of will-power, the differentiator between the conscientious and the lazy, the spark that ignites a flame. Remember that with anything, all gotta start somewhere.

Final points

So yeah, akrasia – what a word. A perfect blend of discipline and gut intuition. I'm glad I found this word as it combines these two fundamentals of self-improvement that I truly value.

Thanks for reading everyone! Let me know your thoughts on this and let's do our best to catch ourselves when we find ourselves becoming gripped in a state of akrasia. It's hard to see any genuine pleasure from being stuck in such a rut like this.

Take care guys.

x
