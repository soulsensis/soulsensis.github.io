A man eating a banana

15 December 2020

He seems nonchalant, but also in deep rumination about something. Perhaps I'm overthinking, who knows. Maybe he might just be completely nonchalant and has absolutely nothing in his mind other than the great taste of the banana he's munching on. In any event, he seems, okay. Neither happy nor sad. Neither ecstatic nor depressed. He seems neutral, and I respect that.

I mean, at the end of the day, what is the point of becoming too concerned about what other people are thinking. They're on their own journey. And you are on your own. The more inward you can focus in your life, the less other people's petty concerns affect you and that, to me, is incredibly powerful.

So, I'm sitting opposite this man eating a banana, and I'm just flabbergasted by all these unyielding attempts at understanding my analysis of him. I actually can't tell what he's thinking but that's okay. People sometimes are just trying to go through life on their own accord without being overly concerned with how other people see him.

What has this man eating a banana taught me? He's taught me that his action of eating and filling himself with nutrition is not any more important than my task of typing this essay on my computer. We are all universally and intrinsically equal in value, spiritually - focus on the essence of this when you interact with any other human being. Respect this integrity, this sense of spirituality that you can invoke which can accurately represent someone.
