The mind is a tool

25 October 2026

You can feel cold in a dream. You can feel the horror of being chased. Then you wake up and call it "just a dream", nothing that's real.

But what exactly is real? Real to us is, and always will be, our perception of the senses. So if a dream is also sensed by our senses, what's to say it isn't real?

In the spiritual realm, the belief is that dreams are our soul's way of exploring other universes, parallel or not. A dream is a space where we can explore our own capabilities, and indulge in our greatest sins. Lucid dreaming doesn't exist for nothing. People intentionally wake up inside their dreams and take control, going anywhere, anytime, at the speed of thought.

Life is infinite. The finite exists only within the mind. Our soul has always been free, and dreaming shows it.

I know this from my own dreams. When I feel lonely, I have dreams of kissing someone. I take it as a signal. My soul is telling me it needs more connection, and I give it that in real life, by being closer to the people around me, by not isolating myself and working on my business all the time. The dream knew what I needed before my mind did.

That's why I trust my gut and go with it. I don't identify with my mind, because the mind is never true. The mind is a tool, and a tool does not tell the truth. All it does is the job the user assigns it. So how can the mind be the truth?

The only truth in my life is my soul, and what it wishes to experience. The question is whether I recognise myself as the soul, and sidestep the mind's justifications, which are swayed by the world significantly.

And the world has plenty to sway it with. With the plethora of distractions, it's not surprising people are lost, and don't know what they're really here on earth to do. Consumption has a numbing effect. It lets people "relax". But true relaxation is being able to sit still in your own damn mind and be ok with yourself. Honestly. There are people right now who are genuinely not ok sitting still with their own minds, and my heart truly goes out to them. That's why this channel exists. It's my tribute to the most important people in my life, which is humanity itself.

Because when you do sit still, the soul gets a word in, and it tells you what you're here for. We are here to experience giving our best selves. So what do you bring best to this world, that you would give for free? Just do it. You'll inspire people to give for free too. And what happens when everyone starts giving for free? We all give, and receive, at greater levels. Right now, so many people are takers.

Yet we live in a society that focuses on the reduction of spirit, the numbness of the soul from truth. So we can't experience a deep and meaningful life. You can hear it in how we talk to each other. Conversations are stale. Who won the footy on the weekend. How's work. How's the weather. Nobody's asking the real questions.

The real questions aren't hard. How are you, really? How's life? Are you taking good care of yourself? What's weighing on you? Do you want to share it? I'm happy to hear you out.

These are the conversations you have with true friends. Friends who can talk about what deeply ails them, and aren't afraid of topics that might be "sensitive" to emotionally detached people.

That's what my soul was asking for in that dream. Someone across the table asking how I really am, and meaning it.

So try this tomorrow. Sit still for five minutes with no phone. Then ask one person how they really are, and hear them out.

Because the more you go within, the more you realise you live in a universe, but the universe also lives inside you.
