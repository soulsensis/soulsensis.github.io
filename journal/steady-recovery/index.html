Steady recovery

18 January 2020

I've been getting better, I think… My recovery from my anxiety and depression has forced me to do things which I normally wouldn't do. One of the major ones is to stop procrastinating. I think I'm generally pretty good at knowing what I SHOULD be doing at a certain point in time. Perhaps all of us have the inner voice which let's us know what we should be doing. I'm actually not sure if this is true, is it a thing? Do you guys have this voice inside you? This awareness or consciousness that's guiding you to do things that better your life and ultimately it's just up to you to decide whether you should act on it?

I've noticed that if I don't follow this inner voice and do what needs to be done, ie. procrastinate, then my anxiety flares up and as a result, plummets me further into depression. I've been feeling a bit out of it lately - not sure why… or do I? Maybe I do know what I need to do and I just can't admit that something is bothering me and that I need to act to rectify it. Further, being blessed with what I can humbly admit to be above average intelligence, I'm able to constantly logically reason my way out of doing anything - it's just so damn easy. The smarter you are, you're not only better at outsmarting others - you're actually also better at outsmarting yourself… lmao how fucked is that hey? LOL

Anyways - recovery is slow, but steady. I got some things I need to do. Need to continue to work on my creative outlet. So yeah, there's quite a bit to focus on for 2020 but let's just do it.

I need to be aware that even slight progress is progress. Even something small like this is progress to me and I'm glad. I use to always consider progress to need to be BIG changes to my life - not just these small simple things. I always like taking big steps but I've realised that I've crashed and burned b/c of this mentality. Fuck. Life is actually pre damn hard man. Or am I just making it difficult for myself? Honestly - I've been a lot more chill lately, trying to take it easy and it works. The only problem is that, whilst I can be chill, I cannot let this translate into complacency and procrastination.
