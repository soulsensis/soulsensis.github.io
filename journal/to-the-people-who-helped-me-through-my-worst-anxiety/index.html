To the people who helped me through my worst anxiety

16 April 2020

I went to Malaysia. It was meant to go for a month but I lasted only 3 days. To this day those 3 days remain a traumatising experience, given the severity of my anxiety. I was in a perpetual state of panic, and was suggestible to so many intrusive thoughts. To outline a few, I kept envisioning myself jumping off buildings. I would also be scared of seeing knives or any sharp objects that may cause me harm. I was stuck on a flight or fight response - unable to eat, nor sleep. I took my medication, but it wasn't enough to get me through.

This post is actually an appreciation post so I truly mean it from the bottom of my heart when I say thank you to the friends I went with who took care of me. Thank you to the boys who I shared the apartment with who made it bearable for me, I don't know where I'd be without you.

Finally thank you dad for flying over to Malaysia to take me back home. I was so broken, so scared, and to see your face just brought me to tears. I was so scared guys.
