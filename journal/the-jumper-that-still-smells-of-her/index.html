The jumper that still smells of her

11 February 2020

Storytime: "I brought the jumper towards my face. The familiar, floral scent of her and her room lingers as I take a deep breath, breathing in the memories of love and laughter. I breathed in our promises of a future together, of having kids together, the names we'll give them should it be a boy or girl, the holidays that will never be lived. I broke down. How could I not. As I drove home, teary eyed, trying to wipe away the tears so I can see the damn road, I could only think of her and how she was able to make me feel like the happiest guy on earth."

"When you cry like this, what gets me the most is the sound I make as I sob away uncontrollably. The sound of despair and desperation. I know I'm healing - and I guess this is unfortunately part of the journey. As I cry, I feel myself getting better. Emotional acknowledgement. I tell myself I'm still healing… I don't know exactly how long this process will be. And it's ok. I'm healing."

"When your way of life is turned upside down, you realise you have to start anew. There's a lot of courage in doing that but right now I just can't see it. All I see are the fragmented pieces of myself scattered throughout the floor and I've only picked up the first piece. I remind myself, hey, at least you're picking them up. At least you're trying to mend yourself together."

"I stopped crying and could comfortably put my foot on the accelerator again since the road came back into vision. Rolling my windows down, sunroof open, blasting Slow Dancing in the Dark by Joji, I stuck out my hand to feel the wind twirl through my fingers and my Buddhist bracelet making clattering noises as the beads resonated with the wind. It was then, that the realisation hit me. I'm that dude. I've gone full sadboi. Oh shit."

"I told myself it is what it is and with the zen of 20,000 monks I proceeded to drive, lane changing without signaling because let's be honest, that's fucking way more sadboi. As I continued to drive, letting go of the thoughts of her, something in me was also letting go. It was my anxiety."

"Right now, it's only me, my music and the road."

Hope you're all taking care

With love,
