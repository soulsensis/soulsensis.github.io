The inflated ego of the self-proclaimed influencer

26 November 2019

I remember being so sucked into this idea cus at the time I had a very inflated ego and just wanted followers. I can also admit that I use to bot which is legit probably one of the most shameful things I've done just to get followers LMAO but hey, give your boy a break - at least I'm admitting it true?

Social media these days has become a platform to showcase your life to your friends. For those who use IG for that I'm all good with it, just don't expect your followers to grow more than your following since this is normal.

However, if you do want to become a real influencer, you gotta start thinking about what kind of value you want to deliver to your audience and perhaps, to the world at large. This has been something that I've been critically ruminating for some time about where I want to go with IG. Even these thought catalogues were a way for me to hopefully deliver some value to people who read them as I hope some of my perspectives on life might assist some people. Even if I assist a few people or a lot of people with these thoughts, I don't mind at all.

My vision is to have a world where people are more genuine, more frank, more honest with each other - basically just cut the bullshit - let's chat. More most people I feel these kind of conversations are reserved for those rare gems you trust in your life but I'm envisioning a possibility where you're able to perhaps have these conversations with anyone. I try to. All the time. It hurts when some don't reciprocate but I've learnt to deal with it better now - but it certainly doesn't demotivate me to keep trying. I want to bring more value to you guys and to the world and will never stop trying to do so. Take care of yourselves guys.
