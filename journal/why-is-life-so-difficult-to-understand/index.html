Why is life so difficult to understand?

15 December 2020

I am grappling with the pain of loneliness and the need to develop myself to be able to face life on my own terms and by myself. It is difficult to come to terms with this idea because I have been so used to having a partner to share my concerns with. The problem was these concerns weren't hers to deal with. These are problems that pre-date my relationship and I needed to solve, or at least, understand it as much I can before I get into a new relationship.

I'm taking my time. I'm taking this opportunity to figure myself out, by myself, with myself. I want to spend this next few months diving deep into my soul to understand what it means to be human, what does it mean to be a good human being in this world, and also, what does it mean to live a good life? Right now, it's just difficult to see and perhaps it is due to my lack of sobriety. So, as I've exhausted my weed stash, it's a good time for this week for me to just focus on what it is that I need to do and make that concerted effort to get my life in order.

This is no easy task. Getting your life in order means you must confront the chaos that presumably exists and sometimes this means you will experience emotions that you do not wish to have to do. In order to grow, you must be at this balanced point where you've got support, yet you also have this wonderful opportunity to go forth and explore the unknown.

I want to decide to take myself seriously. I want to respect myself and in order to do that, there are a lot of things in my life that I need to do.

I no longer want to rely on others for positivity. I want to become the source of it.

I no longer want someone to help me get better. I want to get better myself and be a bedrock for others.

I really care about people, truly. It's a shame that I wasn't this person in the past and I played into mind games that are simply not worth the effort and time. I guess I should attribute this to growth and be done with it.

There is absolutely nothing wrong with admitting your flaws. To grow you must accept that you aren't the best version you can be at this very moment in time - and more importantly, realise that it is okay. Realise that you are not alone in this journey of awakening - this powerful realisation will take me far and I'm for once, very excited to see where this will take me.
