The website

The letters

Signing up gives you one essay a week by email, free. Substack sends them. Unsubscribe any time from the link in any email.

Ask soulsensis

The chat at soulsensis.com/ask is an AI built from the writer's own words. It is a friend's perspective, not a professional one. When it opens, its privacy page will explain what it keeps (almost nothing) and how to delete it.

soulsensis studio

It is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service, the Instagram Terms of Use and the Facebook Terms, for the accounts it connects to.

Content published through the tool is original work by the owner. Where a platform asks whether content is AI-generated or made with AI assistance, the tool says so. The tool is provided as it is, for the owner's own use, with no warranty.
